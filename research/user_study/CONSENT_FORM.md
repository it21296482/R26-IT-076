**Study:** Understanding a stock: does a plain-language explanation help?

## What is this study about?

We are testing whether an explanation produced by our research software helps people understand what happened to a company's share price. You will look at information about two companies listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange and answer short questions on a laptop. It takes about 15 minutes.

## What will you do?

- Choose English, Sinhala or Tamil.
- Look at information about two companies. One is shown as a price chart only; the other also has the system's explanation.
- Answer five short questions about each company, rate how confident you are, and answer a few questions about yourself (age group, whether you have bought shares, how much you know about the stock market).

## Is it a test of me?

No. We are testing the software, not you. There are no penalties for wrong answers, and "Not sure" is always an option.

## What do you record?

Your answers, how long each page took, your age group, language and experience level. **We do not record your name with your answers.** This consent form is stored separately from your answers.

## Can I stop?

Yes. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. If you ask us after the session, we will delete your answers (please keep your participant ID: ______).

## Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond everyday computer use. **Nothing in this study is investment advice.** You may find it interesting to see how stock information can be explained. There is no payment.
