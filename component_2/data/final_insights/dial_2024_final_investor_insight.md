### Risk Factors
- Liquidity Risk: The Group manages liquidity risk through cash flow forecasting and maintaining banking facilities; current ratio remained below 1 at 0.51.
- Credit Risk: Credit risk arises mainly from trade receivables; impairment provisions are recognised using expected credit loss models.
- Market Risk: Exposure to macroeconomic conditions and competitive pressures in the Sri Lankan telecommunications market.
- Interest Rate Risk: The Group has significant borrowings with both fixed and variable interest rates, exposing it to interest rate fluctuations.
- Foreign Exchange Risk: Exposure arises from USD-denominated borrowings and capital expenditure; a 1% USD/LKR movement impacts results materially.
- Operational Risk: Risks related to network reliability, system complexity, and service quality.
- Regulatory Risk: Subject to telecommunications regulations, spectrum allocation decisions, taxation, and compliance requirements.
- Going Concern Concerns: Not found
- Litigation Contingencies: Ongoing legal matter with Sri Lanka Customs relating to historical import duties of equipment, disclosed as a contingent liability.

## Beginner-Friendly Explanation

Dialog Axiata PLC is a leading Sri Lankan telecommunications company. In 2024, it generated Rs. 171 billion in revenue and Rs. 12.4 billion in net profit, showing continued profitability after a recent turnaround. The company invested heavily in network infrastructure and completed the acquisition of Airtel Lanka to strengthen its market position.

### Key Strengths
- Market leadership in Sri Lankan telecommunications
- Strong operating cash flows of Rs. 49.5 billion
- Improved profitability following cost rationalisation
- High dividend payout of 74% for FY2024

### Key Concerns
- Current ratio below 1 indicates tight short-term liquidity
- Exposure to foreign exchange and interest rate movements
- Ongoing contingent litigation with Sri Lanka Customs

### Risk Level Explanation
The company operates in a capital-intensive and regulated industry, with moderate financial and market risks mainly from debt levels, currency exposure, and regulation. These risks are actively managed but remain relevant for investors.
