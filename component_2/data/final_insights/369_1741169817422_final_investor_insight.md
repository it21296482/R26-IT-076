## Beginner-Friendly Explanation

Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC is Sri Lanka’s largest private sector bank by assets. In 2024, it grew total assets to Rs. 2.79 trillion and earned a profit after tax of Rs. 55.7 billion. The Bank operates across multiple banking segments and maintains strong capital and profitability ratios.

### Key Strengths
- Large and diversified banking operations
- Strong profitability with ROE above 22%
- Improved asset quality with lower impaired loan ratios
- Consistent dividend payments combining cash and scrip

### Key Concerns
- Exposure to economic and political conditions in operating countries
- Sensitivity to interest rate and foreign exchange movements
- Credit risk from lending activities

### Risk Level Explanation
As a commercial bank, risk mainly comes from lending, interest rates, currency movements and economic conditions. The Bank manages these through capital buffers, diversification and regular stress testing.
