Why the system is layered this way

Design rationale

What each boundary protects

01

Independent research

Each component owns one evidence stream. This makes the individual research contributions visible and lets the team test a component without hiding failures inside a combined score.

02

Controlled integration

The API validates inputs, coordinates the analysis stages, and keeps uploaded reports and temporary analysis records behind authenticated routes.

03

Evidence before language

The fusion layer combines structured outputs only after the components return their evidence, warnings, and limitations. The interface can then explain the result without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.

04

CSE-focused delivery

The final dashboard brings local market behaviour, company reports, news, and external factors into one view for the Colombo Stock Exchange context.

Research boundaryThe product supports market understanding. It does not provide buy, sell, or hold recommendations.
